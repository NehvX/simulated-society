CIVITAS: GUIDE TO MAKING YOUR OWN MIX OF BEHAVIOUR AND RULES
============================================================

There are two ways to run your own mix:

  EASY WAY:  double-click Civitas.bat, choose option 6, and answer the questions.
  TYPED WAY: python main.py --trait honesty=-0.5 --rule equal_shares --set policy.education_subsidy=0.9

Either way, the results open in your browser automatically (on localhost), and
the full text report is saved in the "out" folder.


1. PEOPLE'S BEHAVIOUR: TRAIT SHIFTS  (--trait NAME=AMOUNT)
----------------------------------------------------------
Shifts the average personality of the whole founding population, measured in
standard deviations (s.d.). The shift is inherited, so children carry it too.

  -1.0  much lower than normal     -0.5  lower     0  normal     +0.5  higher     +1.0  much higher

  honesty            Higher: people cooperate more, cheat and steal less, respect resource
                     quotas, and elect less corrupt mayors. Lower: fraud, bribery,
                     embezzlement and over-harvesting rise.
  agreeableness      Higher: kinder, less violent, take less from the shared resource,
                     better marriages.
  conscientiousness  Higher: more self-control; higher wages, keep jobs longer, save more,
                     healthier and longer-lived, less crime.
  openness           Higher: more curious; more education and innovation (a growth boost),
                     lean politically left, more open-minded in arguments.
  neuroticism        Higher: more anxious; more stress, more mental-health crises, more
                     open to conspiracy theories, less happy.
  intelligence       Higher: more education and higher wages; resists misinformation.
  extraversion       Higher: more social contacts, more charismatic candidates.

Example: a selfish population:   --trait honesty=-0.6 --trait agreeableness=-0.6


2. THE SHARED RESOURCE RULE  (--rule NAME)
------------------------------------------
The town shares a renewable resource (like a fishery or farmland). The rule
decides how much people may take and who receives the value.

  open_access        No limits; everyone keeps what they take. Risk: the tragedy of the
                     commons, where selfish people strip the resource.
  regulated          (default) A sustainable quota per person; keep what you take.
  equal_shares       Quota; the whole harvest is pooled and split equally among adults.
  need_based         Quota; the harvest goes first to people who can't afford essentials.
  private_ownership  Owners (in proportion to wealth) take 60%, workers get 40%. Well
                     protected, but usually more unequal.


3. ANY OTHER SETTING  (--set NAME=VALUE, or "Extra settings" in the menu)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the menu, type several settings separated by spaces, e.g.
    policy.education_subsidy=0.9 policy.police_per_1000=8

Government policy (normal value in brackets):
  policy.income_tax=0.26            tax rate on income above $1,000 a month
  policy.top_tax=0.10               extra tax on income above $10,000 a month
  policy.welfare_floor=900          guaranteed minimum income, $ per month
  policy.ubi=0                      universal basic income per adult, $ per month
  policy.child_benefit=150          $ per child per month
  policy.police_per_1000=5          police officers per 1,000 residents
  policy.education_subsidy=0.5      share of university tuition paid by the state (0 to 1)
  policy.mental_health_pc=15        mental-health budget, $ per resident per month
  policy.public_services_pc=320     schools, health and roads, $ per resident per month
  policy.pension_rate=0.45          pension as a share of the average wage
  policy.inheritance_tax=0.15       tax on estates

The shared resource:
  commons.regen_rate=0.04           how fast it regrows each month (0.025 = fragile, 0.06 = abundant)
  commons.capacity_per_capita=60    how big it is per resident
  commons.unit_value=400            $ earned per unit harvested
  commons.scarcity_cost=0.6         how much dearer essentials get when it runs out (0.6 = +60%)

Economy and crime:
  economy.mean_expansion_months=84  average months between recessions
  crime.deterrence=3                how strongly the risk of punishment deters crime
  crime.theft_sentence=4            prison months for theft (before repeat-offender increases)

Growth (what drives productivity each year):
  growth.human_capital=0.010        payoff from education
  growth.social_capital=0.040       payoff from everyday cooperation
  growth.scarcity=0.040             damage from an exhausted resource

Every parameter is listed with its explanation in civitas/config.py.


4. RUN OPTIONS
--------------
  --years 40          how many years to simulate
  --population 600    size of the founding population
  --seed 42           same seed + same settings = exactly the same history
  --tag my_mix        a label added to the output file names
  --scenario NAME     start from a preset: boom, collapse, tragedy_of_the_commons,
                      ostrom, corruption, knowledge_economy (your own settings are
                      applied on top)


5. READING THE RESULTS
----------------------
At the top of the dashboard you'll see the verdict (Rapid growth, Growth,
Stagnation, Decline or Collapse), what drove it, and what to boost or fix.
The Development tab shows the development index, its six parts, the resource
stock and income over time. Every past result is listed at
http://localhost:<port>/index.html (menu option 9 opens it).
